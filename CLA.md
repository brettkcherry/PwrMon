## Why this is here, plainly

This is the least popular file in the repository and it's worth explaining rather than just
imposing.

Without a CLA, a contribution arrives under GPL-3.0 only, and can't be covered by the
commercial license alongside the rest of the code. Asking afterward means going back to
everyone who ever contributed, or removing their work — both worse than asking once, up front.

If you'd rather not sign, that's a legitimate position and it's genuinely fine. Bug reports,
hardware dumps, reproductions and design feedback need no agreement at all — and
[hardware reports](https://github.com/brettkcherry/PwrMon/issues/new/choose) are the single
most valuable contribution to this project right now regardless.
